Frequently asked

How are aurora alerts sent? Owlhour has no server. Alerts are planned on your iPhone from the forecast and checked again whenever iOS gives the app background time, which can be minutes or hours apart. During a big storm, open the app or start a viewing session (Pro).

Why is the Sky Score low on a clear night? Tap an hour to see what limits it: twilight, a bright Moon, haze or high cloud. The Sky Score is for faint stars and the Milky Way; the aurora chance is separate.

Where does the data come from? Weather from Apple Weather; aurora and Kp from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center; light pollution from VIIRS night lights (Earth Observation Group, Colorado School of Mines, CC BY 4.0); satellite orbits from CelesTrak. Sun, Moon, Milky Way and passes are computed on your iPhone.

Restore a purchase? Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Location? Your location is used on your iPhone to forecast your sky; it is not sent to the developer. You can also pick places by name without sharing your location.
