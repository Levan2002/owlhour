Owlhour

Privacy Policy

Updated 2 October 2026

In short: Owlhour has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your location and saved places stay on your iPhone.

Location and places

If you allow it, Owlhour reads your location while you use the app to forecast your sky. Your location and the places you save are stored only on your iPhone (and in your own encrypted iCloud device backup, if you use it). The developer has no server and never receives them.

To forecast, the app asks Apple's weather service (WeatherKit) for the weather at the coordinates, and Apple Maps for place names and search. These requests go to Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Aurora, solar-wind and satellite data are downloaded from public sources (NOAA SWPC, CelesTrak and a public TLE service) without sending your location.

Notifications and widgets

Alerts are local notifications planned on your iPhone; no push service is used. Widgets read the forecast the app saved on your device.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm your Pro status. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which product, when and its status) and a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device. This data is used only to provide purchases and restores. It is not linked to your identity, not used for tracking and not sold.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete saved places in the Places list, or delete the app to remove everything. To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact the developer.
