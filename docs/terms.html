Forecasts

Sky Scores, aurora chances, light-pollution estimates, satellite passes and astronomy times are forecasts and models. Weather and space weather change quickly and can be wrong. Owlhour is for planning hobby observing and photography; do not rely on it for safety. Take care at night: dress for the cold, tell someone where you go, respect private land and park rules, and drive carefully.
